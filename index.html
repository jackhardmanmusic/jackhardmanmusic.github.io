JACK HARDMAN

Songwriter/Producer/Composer

Jack Hardman is a South London–based writer, producer and composer.

Releasing music under the moniker Good Dog, his single Stay and Rest for a While has surpassed 2 million streams, while its follow-up Hopeless Man topped the iTunes Songwriter Chart.

As a writer–producer with credits spanning Yaelokre, Luke Sital-Singh, Nadia Loren, Exit Stencil and Lexie Carroll, Jack has built a reputation for developing some of the most exciting new artists in the UK.

He also composes for screen, with recent scores including the Channel 4 comedy blap Break Clause, the BAFTA-winning documentary Frontier Town, and the Academy-qualifying short Other Life.
